* About me
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: about
:END:

I'm a postdoc in mathematics at [[https://www.mathinf.uni-heidelberg.de/en][Heidelberg University]] in the research group of [[https://www.mathi.uni-heidelberg.de/~schmidt][Alexander Schmidt]]. \\
Previously, I was a [[https://www.dfg.de/en/research-funding/funding-opportunities/programmes/individual/walter-benjamin][Walter-Benjamin fellow]] based at [[https://www.ihes.fr/][IHES]] in the research group of [[https://www.ihes.fr/en/professeur/dustin-clausen-2/][Dustin Clausen]]. \\
I like to think about arithmetic homotopy theory. \\
